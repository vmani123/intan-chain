# Bench log, 2026-10-13 (overnight)

Single-board bench: Arty S7-25 running `chain_arty_s7_25_div6.bit` (QSPI at
8 MHz), JA-JB jumpers, Pmod JC to an ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 clone, a home router
(2.4 GHz, channel 1), the Mac and the ESP on the same network.

## Result

The basic chain works end to end: emulator -> JA/JB jumpers -> receiver ->
QSPI -> ESP32-S3 -> Wi-Fi -> Mac, bit-exact.

| Run | Rate | Received | Lost | Bit errors | FPGA latency (median) | ESP latency (median / p99) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| emulator, PHASE_SELECT 5 | 2 kS/s | 20 028 frames | 0 | 0 of 41.0 M | 1144 us | 15 / 210 us |
| emulator, PHASE_SELECT 6 | 2 kS/s | 20 005 frames | 0 | 0 of 41.0 M | 1144 us | 15 / 195 us |
| emulator, PHASE_SELECT 5 | 4 kS/s | 40 000 frames | 0 | 0 of 81.9 M | 644 us | 20 / 345 us |
| emulator, PHASE_SELECT 5 | 8 kS/s | 62 600 frames | 20.6 % | 0 of 128.2 M | 394 us | 8680 / 13 497 us |
| internal pattern (3 runs) | 2 kS/s | 21 861 frames | 0 | 0 of 44.8 M | | |

- PHASE_SELECT scan through the jumpers: 5 and 6 recover all 128 channels on
  every frame; 0-4 and 7-11 recover none. Simulation predicted 3-4; the extra
  two quarter-SCLK steps are pad and jumper delay.
- 8 kS/s is past what this Wi-Fi link carries cleanly (the ESP alone peaked at
  21.5 Mbit/s, ~10.5 kS/s, with 0.8 % air loss). Every frame that arrived was
  exact; the loss is the radio, and the ESP queue shows it (p99 13.5 ms).
  The clean ceiling on this network is somewhere between 4 and 8 kS/s.
- The FPGA latency is mostly time spent filling a datagram: a frame waits for
  the other four of its datagram, so it halves as the rate doubles.

## What was wrong, and what changed

1. **ESP: the IOMUX route never decoded a command.** All six QSPI lines were
   live at the ESP, but the slave flagged `cmd_err` on every transaction and
   never saw WR_DONE. A pin-assignment scan (`CONFIG_CHAIN_QSPI_PIN_SCAN`)
   showed the wiring matched the plan exactly, and that through the GPIO
   matrix full 1380-byte segments closed. Fix: the slave is forced through the
   GPIO matrix (`SPICOMMON_BUSFLAG_GPIO_PINS`). ESP-IDF says the two routes
   behave the same below 80 MHz, so the cause is still open.
2. **ESP: the receiver could deadlock.** If the buffer pool ran dry with
   nothing armed, it never re-armed: ready stayed low and the FPGA stalled for
   good (24 datagrams, then nothing, at 8 kS/s). Fix: re-arm on a 2 ms timer
   while short of buffers.
3. **Host: CHECK scoring assumed the pattern restarts with seq.** It does not
   (FPGA behaviour, below), so every run after the first scored ~50 % bit
   errors on perfect data. Fix: `metrics` finds the per-run offset.
4. **Host: stale frames looked like 4 billion lost frames.** The first datagram
   of a run can hold the previous run's last frames. Fix: they are dropped and
   counted as `stale_frames`.
5. **Host: the first datagrams of a run were lost** because the socket opened
   after the FPGA started. Fix: `receiver.open_socket()` before `set_run`.
6. **Host: `--phase-scan` could never work.** The sweep always set MODE bit 2
   (internal pattern), where PHASE_SELECT does nothing. Fix: `--source
   emulator` scores against the emulator's memory, and `--phase-scan` uses it.
7. **The `screen` instructions were wrong.** The board ends commands on line
   feed only: press Ctrl-J, not Enter.

## FPGA items for when synthesis is available again

Nothing here blocks the bench; the host works around both.

- `clear_counters` resets seq but not the pattern generator. Either reset the
  generator on clear (and on run start), or define in CONTRACTS.md that the
  index is free-running and let the host keep finding the offset.
- A partly built datagram survives a stop/start. Flush or discard the partial
  slot when `run` falls.
- If the IOMUX question turns out to be edge rate: `SLEW SLOW` / `DRIVE 4` on
  `qspi_sclk` in `chain_arty_s7_25.xdc` is the cheap experiment.

## Sharing the ESP32-S3

The "ESP-S3/C5 wireless study" session also uses this S3. Agreed protocol:
the board belongs to whoever holds the directory
`~/.esp32s3-usbmodem5B8F0676581.lock` (take it with `mkdir`, release with
`rm -rf`), and whoever takes it reflashes their own firmware first. That study
also needs the 2.4 GHz air quiet while it measures. The S3 stays wired to the
Arty either way: its firmware leaves GPIO9-14 and 21 alone.

The Arty's bitstream is volatile: after a power cycle, reload it with
`openFPGALoader -b arty_s7_25 ~/Downloads/chain_arty_s7_25_div6.bit`.
