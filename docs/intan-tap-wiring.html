Intan tap wiring

The Intan controller keeps driving the headstage through two SPI cables spliced by a pair of C3430 adapter boards. The Arty is wired to board Y and listens to all five LVDS pairs without driving any of them, then sends what it hears over the usual QSPI, ESP32-C5 and Wi-Fi path, so the Mac ends up with two recordings of the same samples.

branch intan-tap-check · docs/intan-tap-check.md · 2026-11-02

Never

  • Connect Y B6 (cable power) to anything but X T6.
  • Use any Pmod 3V3 pin (6 or 12). Only ground is shared.
  • Load the headstage, chain or emulator bitstream while the tap is plugged in. They drive JA1–JA3 as 3.3 V outputs into the cable's CS and SCLK pairs.

Meter before power

  • Continuity on the 12 X to Y wires and the 11 Y to Pmod wires.
  • No short inside a pair, between neighbouring pairs, or power to ground.
  • Pass-through alone first: RHX must record exactly as with one cable.

Power-up order

  1. Tap unplugged: power the Arty, load the tap bitstream, check tapcheck status shows the tap.
  2. Controller off: plug the tap into JA and JB, ground first.
  3. Controller on, start RHX.

After any Arty power cycle, unplug the tap before it boots.
